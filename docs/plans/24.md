## Goal

Replace pytz with the standard-library zoneinfo throughout IncidentDesk and its tests, preserve existing timezone behavior including daylight-saving transitions, and remove pytz from the dependencies.

## Steps

1. **Core and UTC paths (about 17 files):** Migrate `incidentdesk/timeutil.py`, `incidentdesk/config.py`, `incidentdesk/models.py`, `incidentdesk/seed.py`, `incidentdesk/cli.py`, `doctor.py`, `incidentdesk/sla.py`, `incidentdesk/escalation/timers.py`, `incidentdesk/api/incidents.py`, `incidentdesk/api/timeline.py`, `incidentdesk/audit/trail.py`, and `incidentdesk/retention/cleanup.py`; update `tests/conftest.py`, `tests/unit/test_timeutil.py`, `tests/unit/test_audit.py`, `tests/unit/test_escalation.py`, and `tests/unit/test_retention.py` in the same step. Keep UTC conversion and defaults equivalent, and verify any local-wall-time helper behavior around DST.
2. **Local schedules, reporting boundaries, and ingest (about 15 files):** Migrate `incidentdesk/maintenance/windows.py`, `incidentdesk/oncall/schedule.py`, `incidentdesk/oncall/rotation.py`, `incidentdesk/oncall/handoff.py`, `incidentdesk/notify/digest.py`, `incidentdesk/reports/daily.py`, `incidentdesk/reports/weekly.py`, `incidentdesk/ingest/email_parser.py`, `incidentdesk/ingest/webhook.py`, and `incidentdesk/ingest/dedupe.py`; update `tests/unit/test_maintenance.py`, `tests/unit/test_oncall.py`, `tests/unit/test_notify.py`, `tests/unit/test_reports.py`, and `tests/unit/test_ingest.py` with their modules. Preserve wall-clock scheduling across DST and explicitly test ambiguous or nonexistent local times where the existing behavior applies.
3. **Remaining displays and dependency cleanup (about 11 files):** Migrate `incidentdesk/exporters/csv_export.py`, `incidentdesk/notify/chat.py`, `incidentdesk/notify/email.py`, `incidentdesk/reports/mttr.py`, `incidentdesk/statuspage/history.py`, and `incidentdesk/statuspage/publisher.py`; update `tests/unit/test_exporters.py`, `tests/unit/test_statuspage.py`, `tests/unit/test_priority.py`, and `tests/unit/test_routing.py`. Once no pytz imports or references remain, remove pytz from `pyproject.toml`.

## Tests

- After each step, run its focused test modules through `scripts/test.sh` so they pass under both UTC and Asia/Singapore.
- Verify DST-sensitive behavior: local wall-clock schedules and day/week boundaries retain their intended local times and durations, including 23- and 25-hour days; exercise ambiguous/nonexistent local times to confirm the chosen zoneinfo `fold`/gap behavior matches the previous behavior.
- After the final step, search the repository for remaining pytz imports/references and run `scripts/test.sh` for the complete suite.

## Risks

- `zoneinfo` does not provide pytz's `localize()` or `normalize()`, and its handling of ambiguous or nonexistent local times can differ. The relevant tests may expose differences in wall-clock schedules, customer-reported times, and reporting boundaries.
- Removing pytz before all references are migrated would break imports; defer dependency removal until the final step and verify no references remain.
- System timezone differences can mask regressions; use `scripts/test.sh` for both timezone environments after every batch.

## Out of scope

- Do not change incident, scheduling, reporting, or API semantics beyond matching the current pytz behavior.
- Do not introduce a third-party timezone dependency or change unrelated tests and configuration.
