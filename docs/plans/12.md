## Goal
Customer-facing incidents are raised one priority level regardless of when they are opened.

## Steps
1. Update `incidentdesk/priority.py` so the customer-facing priority adjustment no longer depends on business hours, while preserving maintenance adjustments and priority bounds.
2. Update `tests/unit/test_priority.py` with separate cases for each documented priority-table example: Tuesday 14:00, Tuesday 23:30, and Saturday 10:00.
3. Add a plain-language entry under `## Unreleased` in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Tests
- Tuesday 14:00, major `login-service`: assert P1 in `tests/unit/test_priority.py`.
- Tuesday 23:30, major `login-service`: assert P1 in `tests/unit/test_priority.py`.
- Saturday 10:00, major `login-service`: assert P1 in `tests/unit/test_priority.py`.
- Confirm routing remains unchanged by running `tests/unit/test_routing.py` without modifying it.
- Run `tests/unit/test_priority.py` to verify the adjustment and existing maintenance and severity behavior.

## Risks
Removing the business-hours check from the priority adjustment could accidentally affect routing; the unchanged routing tests will catch changes to responder selection. Maintenance lowering and P1/P4 bounds must remain intact, as verified by the existing priority tests.

## Out of scope
Stored incidents, routing behavior, and `is_business_hours` are unchanged.
